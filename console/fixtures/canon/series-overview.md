# Series Overview: DeadLight (fixture)

DeadLight follows a skeleton crew running an off-grid relay station after a
signal blackout severs their contact with the mainland. Each season tracks
one relay cycle: the crew fights to keep the station alive while slowly
uncovering who — or what — cut the signal in the first place.

**Tone**: slow-burn survival mystery with dry crew-banter cold opens.
**Format**: 10-episode seasons, ~18 minutes each.
